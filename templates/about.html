From a single warehouse to a continental empire.

GrandMark Distributors was founded on a simple premise: East Africa's rapidly expanding markets required a logistics partner capable of handling immense volume without sacrificing operational integrity. We started as a specialized procurement firm in Nairobi, dealing directly with international manufacturers to source wholesale goods.

Today, GrandMark commands one of the most sophisticated physical supply chains on the continent. We own and operate dedicated ocean freight lanes, thousands of miles of trucking routes, and massive climate-controlled fulfillment centers. We don't rely on third-party brokers; we control the entire lifecycle of the cargo from the factory floor to the final retail destination.

Our Mission

To provide unbreakable, end-to-end supply chain infrastructure that allows African businesses to scale globally with zero logistical friction.

Our Vision

To serve as the undisputed circulatory system for East African commerce, delivering goods faster, safer, and more transparently than any competitor.

Our Journey of Expansion

2015

The Foundation

GrandMark opens its first 50,000 sq.ft warehousing facility in Nairobi, focusing on B2B procurement for local electronics retailers.

2018

Mombasa Port Integration

Acquired direct freight forwarding licenses, establishing our massive ocean-freight clearing hub at the Port of Mombasa.

2022

The Heavy Fleet

Launched a fleet of 500+ GPS-tracked commercial haulers, expanding our distribution network into Uganda and Rwanda.

2026 & BEYOND

Pan-African Dominance

Deploying automated robotics in our mega-hubs and establishing direct air-cargo charter lanes across the entire continent.
