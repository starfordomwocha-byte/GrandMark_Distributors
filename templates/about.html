Kenya's Premier Wholesale & Logistics Network.

Our Sourcing Strategy: We believe that Kenyan retailers deserve access to the best products without the headache of international freight, customs, or factory negotiations. GrandMark leverages deep industry relationships to source top-tier products from across the globe, while proudly championing local Kenyan manufacturers like Bamburi, Kenchic, and Bidco.

Our Distribution: Once products hit our Nairobi distribution center, our localized logistics network takes over. We provide seamless, last-mile delivery to supermarkets, hardware stores, and corporate institutions across the country, ensuring your shelves are never empty.

Our Mission

To provide unbreakable, end-to-end supply chain infrastructure that bridges global manufacturers with local Kenyan businesses effortlessly.

Our Vision

To serve as the undisputed circulatory system for East African commerce, delivering goods faster, safer, and more transparently than any competitor.

Our Journey of Expansion

2015

The Foundation

GrandMark opens its first 50,000 sq.ft warehousing facility in Nairobi, focusing on B2B procurement for local electronics retailers.

2018

Mombasa Port Integration

Acquired direct freight forwarding licenses, establishing our massive ocean-freight clearing hub at the Port of Mombasa.

2022

The Heavy Fleet

Launched a fleet of 500+ GPS-tracked commercial haulers, expanding our local distribution network into all 47 Kenyan counties.

2026 & BEYOND

Pan-African Dominance

Deploying automated robotics in our mega-hubs and establishing direct air-cargo charter lanes across the entire continent.
